Nature Engineering


Nature engineering, or biomimicry, involves designing technologies and systems inspired by natural processes, organisms, and structures to solve complex human challenges. Overview Nature-inspired engineering draws on millions of years of evolution to create efficient, resilient, and innovative solutions. Engineers study animals, plants, and ecosystems to replicate their strategies in human technology, often resulting in more sustainable and effective designs. This approach spans multiple disciplines, including robotics, materials science, construction, transportation, and medical devices.
