Article: AI Transportation in Bengaluru

AI transportation can help Bengaluru by studying traffic flow, bus arrival times, pedestrian safety, and road congestion. If traffic signals respond to real-time conditions, commuters can spend less time waiting. If bus and metro data are easier to access, citizens can plan better trips and reduce private vehicle use.

Smart mobility should also protect privacy and include people who do not use smartphones. A good smart city is not only digital; it is inclusive, safe, clean, and simple for citizens to use.

FAQ Section

What is a smart city?

A smart city uses planning, technology, data, and citizen participation to improve public services.

Is Bengaluru the same as Bangalore?

Yes. Bengaluru is the official name, while Bangalore is still commonly used in conversation and older documents.
