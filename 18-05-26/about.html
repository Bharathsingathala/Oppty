City Vision

The Bengaluru smart city vision focuses on clean mobility, better streets, public safety, digital services, environmental care, and inclusive urban development.

BBMP supports civic administration, while BMRCL supports metro transport through Namma Metro.

AI can help planners understand congestion, predict demand, and respond faster to incidents.

Important Goals

  1. Increase public transport use.
  2. Improve footpaths and accessible streets.
  3. Use technology for transparent citizen services.
  4. Protect lakes, trees, parks, and public spaces.
  5. Support inclusive development for all citizens.

Smart Governance for Bengaluru

Smart governance means citizens should be able to find information quickly, report problems easily, and receive updates clearly. A city portal can collect services in one place and reduce confusion for students, workers, visitors, and residents.
